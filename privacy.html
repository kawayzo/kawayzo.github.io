Privacy Policy

yt-analytics · last updated 12 October 2026

What the app is

yt-analytics is a private, single-user tool. Its author uses it to read analytics for YouTube channels they own. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the account owner's permission, granted through Google sign-in, the app requests read-only access to:

  • YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting data (views, watch time, impressions, click-through rate, audience retention, traffic sources, audience age, gender and country in aggregate);
  • basic public information about the owner's own channel and videos (titles, publish dates, view counts).

The app cannot upload, change or delete anything on YouTube or in the Google account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to produce reports for the channel owner about their own videos. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Where the data is stored

Reports and the sign-in token are stored only on the owner's personal computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

How to revoke access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data can be deleted by the owner at any time.
